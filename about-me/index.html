about me

Growing up dancing, ballet flats take me back to where I learned discipline, creativity, and communication

Hi, I’m Bella! I’m a student at the University of Southern California pursuing a BSc in Business Administration with an emphasis in Risk Management and an intended MS in Product Development Engineering.

My work lies in the intersection of technology and strategy: understanding what technology can enable and understanding the people, businesses, and problems that ultimately determine how it creates value. I've explored this through product and marketing work at Crusoe and ASE Technology Holding, and also through strategy consulting work for organizations including Omni Hotels & Resorts and Los Angeles World Airports.

But at the core of all of this is my love for learning, especially when it means stepping into something I don’t fully understand yet. It’s what led me from business to engineering, across industries and around the world. I’m always looking for the next opportunity to challenge what I know, learn from people with different perspectives, and leave with a slightly bigger understanding of the world than I had before.

Pro-Bono ConsultantOmni Hotels+

Omni Hotels & Resorts wanted to evaluate how its food and beverage operations could transition from a guest amenity into a scalable, profit-driving business nationwide. The project focused on diagnosing revenue leakage from discounting, inconsistent pricing practices, and fragmented operational data, as well as identifying opportunities to increase check size, local diner capture, and repeat visitation. Our team developed a comprehensive strategy spanning menu engineering, AI-enabled pricing tools, discount governance, staff training, and digital guest engagement. The recommendations established a national framework to sustainably grow F&B revenue while preserving Omni’s luxury brand positioning.

Pro-Bono Consulting Project ManagerLA General+

LA General Medical Center asked our team to redesign its wayfinding system across a complex, multi-building campus serving a diverse patient population. The project addressed navigation challenges that disproportionately affected first-time visitors, non-English speakers, elderly patients, and individuals with disabilities. By mapping the full patient journey from pre-arrival to appointment, we identified breakdowns in communication, signage, and digital tools. Our recommendations centered on a hybrid wayfinding model combining multilingual pre-arrival communication, ADA-compliant physical signage, and digital mapping solutions to reduce confusion, improve punctuality, and enhance overall patient experience without requiring major structural changes.

Strategic Marketing & Planning InternASE+

ASE Technology Holding is the world’s largest outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) provider, operating at a critical stage of the global semiconductor value chain. Within the Strategic Marketing and Planning team, I worked on market and industry analysis to understand emerging opportunities, competitive dynamics, and the broader trends shaping the semiconductor industry.

Pro-Bono ConsultantLA International Airport+

Los Angeles World Airports partnered with our team to support the launch of the Landside Access Modernization Program (LAMP) and prepare for significant passenger influxes ahead of the 2028 Olympics and World Cup. Our work evaluated digital and in-person communication strategies, innovative accessibility technologies, and global best practices from prior Olympic host airports. We delivered a phased strategy encompassing inclusive marketing, partnerships with disability advocacy groups, wayfinding innovations, staff training, and event-ready operational planning to position LAX as a global leader in accessible and inclusive air travel.
