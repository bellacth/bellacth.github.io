CompanyCentral Pattana / centralwOrld

LocationBangkok, Thailand

Transforming into a flagship mall for Gen Z in Thailand

Problem diagnosis

centralwOrld already offers the retail, dining, entertainment, and work destinations Gen Z wants, and yet, it still hasn't established itself as the preferred "3rd place" for Gen Z. It lacks memorable experiences connecting these spaces and has little room for major development due to high occupancy.

Solution

So we recommended mOments — a network of free, nostalgia-inspired digital games placed in underutilized walkways, using Central X to capture memories, encourage exploration and repeat visits, and create sponsorship and sales opportunities for tenants.
