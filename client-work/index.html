LA General Medical Center

Patient-centric wayfinding system

Problem diagnosis

Los Angeles General Medical Center asked our team to redesign its wayfinding system across a complex, multi-building campus serving a diverse patient population. The project addressed navigation challenges that disproportionately affected first-time visitors, non-English speakers, elderly patients, and individuals with disabilities. By mapping the full patient journey from pre-arrival to appointment, we identified breakdowns in communication, signage, and digital tools.

Solution

Our recommendations centered on a hybrid wayfinding model combining multilingual pre-arrival communication, ADA-compliant physical signage, and digital mapping solutions to reduce confusion, improve punctuality, and enhance overall patient experience without requiring major structural changes.

02

Strategy Consultant

Omni Hotels & Resorts

Food & beverage revenue optimization

Problem diagnosis

Omni Hotels & Resorts wanted to evaluate how its food and beverage operations could transition from a guest amenity into a scalable, profit-driving business nationwide. The project focused on diagnosing revenue leakage from discounting, inconsistent pricing practices, and fragmented operational data, as well as identifying opportunities to increase check size, local diner capture, and repeat visitation.

Solution

Our team developed a comprehensive strategy spanning menu engineering, AI-enabled pricing tools, discount governance, staff training, and digital guest engagement. The recommendations established a national framework to sustainably grow F&B revenue while preserving Omni’s luxury brand positioning.

03

Associate

Los Angeles World Airports (LAX)

Accessible mobility & passenger experience

Problem diagnosis

Los Angeles World Airports partnered with our team to support the launch of the Landside Access Modernization Program (LAMP) and prepare for significant passenger influxes ahead of the 2028 Olympics and World Cup. The engagement focused on improving accessibility and awareness for travelers with disabilities across the end-to-end airport journey.

Solution

Our work evaluated digital and in-person communication strategies, innovative accessibility technologies, and global best practices from prior Olympic host airports. We delivered a phased strategy encompassing inclusive marketing, partnerships with disability advocacy groups, wayfinding innovations, staff training, and event-ready operational planning to position LAX as a global leader in accessible and inclusive air travel.

04

Project Analyst

CHAGEE

Market expansion & brand positioning

Problem diagnosis

CHAGEE, a premium Chinese tea brand, partnered with our team to evaluate how it could successfully enter the U.S. market while preserving its cultural heritage and premium positioning. The project focused on understanding U.S. consumer perceptions of tea, identifying target demographics, and defining how aesthetics, storytelling, and omnichannel engagement influence trial and retention.

Solution

Through consumer surveys, case studies, and an on-campus activation, we developed a go-to-market strategy that emphasized visual identity, experiential marketing, strategic collaborations, and digital content to redefine tea culture for Gen Z and Millennial consumers.
